# Liang Cheng reference

Detailed English story references, confirmed evolution milestones, and original illustrated form atlases for the Liang Cheng adaptation.

- `seasons-2-to-4.md`: Complete combined reference for Seasons 2–4, with 38 chronological story chapters and 21 original stills. Recommended for reading or AI ingestion.
- `seasons-2-and-3.md`: Separate reference for Seasons 2 and 3.
- `season-4.md`: Separate reference for Season 4.
- `images/`: Original images embedded by the documents. Keep this directory beside them when copying.

The stills retain their original captions and graphics. Body transformations, strength stages, and elemental attachments are identified separately. Uncertain names and inconsistent numbers are explicitly described. The season boundaries follow the reviewed video compilations, without an unverified mapping to individual episode numbers.
