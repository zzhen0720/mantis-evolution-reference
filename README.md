# Mantis Evolution Reference

Flint begins life small enough to hide from a bird in a tree hollow. By the time the beetles of Insectsong Grassland hunt him, he can cut through their commanders' armor.

`story.md` follows his hunts in order, from the primeval jungle to the entrance of the Nine Domains Sacred Land. The form guide pairs ten illustrations with descriptions of his armor, blades, wings, and abilities. The illustrations are in `images/`.
